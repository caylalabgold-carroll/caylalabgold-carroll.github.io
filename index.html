About Me

I’m a Journalism and Political Science student at Northwestern University’s Medill School of Journalism with a passion for telling political and community stories through images

I've worked as a credentialed photographer on Capitol Hill and at the White House for Medill News Service, including covering the 2026 State of the Union. I have also served as Photo Editor, Creative Director and Illustrations Editor at The Daily Northwestern.
